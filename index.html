About the Designer

Sarah Nolasco Perez is a Graphic Design student at the University of Georgia, pursuing a New Media Certificate and building a strong foundation at the intersection of creativity, technology, and visual storytelling. Academically committed and professionally engaged, she approaches her work with intention, empathy, and a clear focus on preparing for a meaningful career in design. Raised in Macon, Georgia, Sarah grew up surrounded by creativity, where design was a part of her everyday life—an influence that sparked her love for visual storytelling from an early age. As a Salvadoran and Mexican designer, she brings cultural awareness, warmth, and perspective to her work, always considering the people on the other side of the screen or page. She strives to create designs that connect people and inspire genuine joy. Whether through thoughtful layouts, playful typography, or expressive color choices, her goal is to craft visual experiences that feel welcoming, purposeful, and uplifting.

Sarah’s experience as a Teacher Assistant at Stone Edge Prep strengthened her communication, organization, and teamwork skills—qualities she carries into every design project. She holds an Adobe InDesign Certificate from LinkedIn Learning and a Prosolution Training Certificate, reflecting her commitment to developing both technical proficiency and professional reliability. Known for being responsible, punctual, and collaborative, she values strong work ethic and clear communication in all her endeavors.

Above all, Sarah is motivated by the belief that good design can bring people together and create moments of genuine happiness. She aspires to create work that not only looks beautiful but also makes others feel seen, inspired, and at ease.
